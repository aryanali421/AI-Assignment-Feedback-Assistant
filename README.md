# AI-Assignment-Feedback-Assistant# Markwise – Educator Workspace

**Better feedback. More meaningful learning.**

Markwise is an AI-assisted assessment workspace that helps educators evaluate student submissions against an assessment rubric. It is designed to produce structured, criterion-specific feedback that educators can review, edit, and share.

## Core workflow

1. **Assessment details** – Add assignment information to give the evaluation its context.
2. **Assessment rubric** – Paste the assessment rubric, including criteria, descriptions, and marks.
3. **Submission evaluation** – Evaluate student work against the supplied assessment context and rubric.
4. **Review and share** – Review the generated feedback, make edits, and share the final feedback.

## Intended use

Markwise is intended to support educator judgement, not replace it. Educators should check the evaluation, confirm that feedback is fair and supported by the submission, and make any necessary corrections before sharing it with a student.

## Important notes

- The quality of the evaluation depends on the clarity and completeness of the assignment details and rubric.
- AI-generated marks and feedback should be treated as recommendations until reviewed by the educator.
- Do not upload confidential student information unless your deployment and data-handling arrangements permit it.
